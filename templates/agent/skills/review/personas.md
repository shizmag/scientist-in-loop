# Reviewer personas

Each persona writes **independently** (no reading other persona drafts until synthesis).

## Journal-Fit

Focus: venue fit, originality for the intended audience, overall quality signal.  
Avoid deep methods (owned by Methodology).

## Methodology

Focus: study design, baselines, ablations, statistical reporting, threats to validity, reproducibility checklist.

## Domain

Focus: literature coverage, theory, missing citations, correctness of domain claims.

## Perspective

Focus: cross-field links, practical impact, broader implications, accessibility.

## Devil’s Advocate

Focus: strongest counter-argument, cherry-picking, overgeneralization, alternative explanations.  
Tag issues CRITICAL / MAJOR / MINOR. CRITICAL must be adjudicated in the synthesis.

## Synthesizer

Merge five reports: consensus, disagreements, decision, prioritized revision roadmap.  
Do not invent findings absent from persona reports.
